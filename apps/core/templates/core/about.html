Rooted in Agriculture. Driven by Technology.

Mabatini Pumba Center (MPC) is your trusted local hub for premium livestock supplies and modern digital web solutions, located at Leganga opposite JOBIG Trading Center and Leganga Gym, Usa-River, Arusha Tanzania.

Our Mission

At MPC, we understand that growth requires the right inputs—whether you are raising livestock or scaling a business online. We started with a simple goal: to provide the community with the highest quality animal feed and agricultural essentials without the usual friction. Zero Friction solutions.

But the modern world moves fast. Recognizing the need for digital efficiency, we expanded our expertise. Today, we don't just supply your farm; we build the custom e-commerce systems, responsive websites, and API integrations that help local businesses thrive in the digital economy.

What We Offer;

Livestock & Agriculture

  • Premium quality Pumba (Bran) and animal feeds.
  • Essential agricultural supplies and tools.
  • Reliable stock availability for local farmers.
